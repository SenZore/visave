Supported sites

visave uses yt-dlp for site downloads. This is a searchable snapshot of its extractor list, grouped by name. An extractor is a way to read a site's media; several entries can belong to one website.

Start with YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Vimeo, Twitch recordings, Facebook, Reddit, or X. Availability depends on the video and your access. Direct video files can work on other sites too.
